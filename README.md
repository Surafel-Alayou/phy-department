# Bahir Dar University Physics Department Website

**Link:** https://surafel-alayou.github.io/phy-department/

## Overview

The **Bahir Dar University Physics Department Website** is an online platform developed to strengthen the department's digital presence and provide a central source of information for students, academic staff, prospective applicants, and visitors.

The website presents the department's academic programmes, research activities, laboratory facilities, staff profiles, and departmental announcements. It also provides students with access to useful academic resources, including books, research projects, theses, examination preparation materials, and scholarship opportunities.

## Key Features

- Centralised access to departmental information and announcements.
- Information about academic programmes, staff, research, and laboratory facilities.
- Academic resources and materials to support students.
- Information on scholarships and further-study opportunities.
- Updates on departmental events and activities.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS and JavaScript
- **Framework:** Bootstrap

## Purpose and Impact

The platform makes departmental information and academic resources more accessible, improves communication, and supports students and staff in their academic activities.
